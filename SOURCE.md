## Type

- Serif: **Test Tiempos Text** (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold), a commercial face (Klim). Used for every
  headline and brand line, in sentence case with tight leading (about 1.05), never all caps.
- Sans: an unnamed light-to-regular grotesque for small text, contacts, the palette labels and the project
  sheet; slightly tight tracking; sentence case. Contacts are set very small in stacked blocks of three lines.
- Habits: headlines broken into two or three short lines; an arrow before the web address ("→ sahra.sa"); small
  text in sand or tribal brown on light, beige on dark; generous empty space.

## Shapes and components

- **Word-mark:** Latin "Sahra" and Arabic "صحراء" joined into one line of equal-weight strokes on a grid. Stroke
  width is the unit **x**; counters and gaps are x; small steps are **y**; all diagonal cuts share one angle (the stills print "34°" on the Latin cuts and "42°" on the Arabic cuts; on the artwork they measure 45°);
  corners are quarter circles on a grid of concentric radii; (measured in step 4 on m04 at 2800 px: stroke 32 px, cut-topped stems 35, bowl and corner radii 72.5 / 40.5, the hah's corners 54 / 22; the cuts drawn in m03, labelled "34°" (Latin) and "42°" (Arabic), measure 45° on the artwork itself, and the red guide lines in m03 also run at 45°) the Arabic letters carry two short bars above; a
  small ring with an open centre sits above the join.
- **Mark:** the "S" of the word-mark alone: two interlocking brackets, a rounded top-left corner and a rounded
  bottom-right corner, each arm ending in an angled cut, with a gap of y between them. Also used inside a rounded
  beige tile as the profile icon.
- **Quarter arch:** a thick quarter ring, used as a window that crops a photograph, as the header shape of the
  profile card, and as the large rounded corner cutting the hoarding photo.
- Buttons: rounded rectangles with a small radius (about 8 px on a 40 px tall button): tribal brown with beige
  text ("→ sahra.sa"), black with white text ("Follow"). Rows on the web page: thin rules, a number, a serif
  label and a "+" at the right. Cards: large radius (about 40 px on the profile card). Print pieces are
  square-cornered.
- Finishes: blind deboss of the word-mark on brown board; painted card edges; fine paper grain on every surface.
- Photographs are warm architecture: curved brick and timber walls, a patch of sky, hard sun and deep shadow.
  They are never reproduced: use flat fields, gradients in the photo tones and the quarter arch.

## Motion personality

Slow, architectural and exact: lines draw on along the grid, shapes slide into place and stop without bounce,
shadows lengthen like sun on a wall. Durations 240 ms (small), 480 ms (standard), 900 ms (large draws); easing
out-quart style curves, for example cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) for entering and cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)
for moves; no overshoot.

## Brand idea (our words, from this case study only)

Sahra treats real estate as careful architecture for living, not a transaction: every space is drawn with
intention, the way its word-mark is drawn on a grid of equal strokes and exact angled cuts. It turns the desert's
own tones and hard light into calm, refined places, joining Arabic and Latin, heritage and modern life, in one
line.
