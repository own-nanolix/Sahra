# LAYOUTS: one line per page, one line per mockup

Direction for this project (from `rule/09-sahra.md`, section 2b):

- **Mockup staging:** in context: each object stands in a simple drawn scene (desk, wall, street, counter, shelf).
- **Mockup backdrop:** a flat field of one brand colour with a hard horizon line (`BackdropG`; no sweep, no
  vignette). Light from the upper left, hard shadows to the lower right, as in the flat lays.
- **Brand pages lean on:** a bento grid of unequal tiles (`bento`, `span-*`, `rows-*`), a different grid on every page.
- **Dashboard option A is:** top navigation over one hero chart; B to E are four other structures.
- **Landing pages lean on:** a long story scroll with visuals that stay pinned beside the text (`story`,
  `story-pin`, `story-steps`); seven different structures.

Rules: where the case study shows a layout, the case study wins (the flat lays m01 / m10, the posters m11 / m14,
the hoarding m16, the box m13, the palette m05, the type slide m06, the colour versions m09, the About page m12).
Nothing repeats: no two lines below may be built the same way. Builders follow their lines; reviewers check
against them.

simple drawn scene part, the object, its materials, a hard shadow to the lower right (`CastG`, `CastRectG`) and a
soft contact shadow where it stands (`ContactShadowG`). The arrangement and camera differ every time.
